Format

  1. Every fixture between two teams is played over 2 legs — a home leg and an away leg.
  2. The home side picks their in-game team first each leg, so across the two legs both sides get a first pick.
  3. The two legs use 4 unique in-game teams — whatever a player picked in leg 1 can't be picked again in leg 2.
  4. The top 2 from each group advance to the knockout bracket.
